Stefano Bernagozzi

Robotics engineer and postdoctoral researcher at the Italian Institute of Technology.

I work on reliable robot autonomy, human-aware planning, behavior trees, and verification and monitoring for autonomous systems. My experience spans research and development with ROS, ROS 2, and YARP, and applications including museum guide robots and human-robot collaboration.
