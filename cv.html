Curriculum vitae · October 2026

Stefano Bernagozzi

Robotics engineer and postdoctoral researcher building dependable, human-aware autonomous systems.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Humanoid Sensing and Perception group at the Italian Institute of Technology (IIT), Genoa. My research combines robot planning, behaviour trees, and verification and runtime monitoring to make autonomous behaviour easier to specify, understand, and trust in real environments.
