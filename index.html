Robotics engineer · Researcher

Hi, I’m Stefano.

I’m a postdoctoral researcher at the Italian Institute of Technology, working on reliable robot autonomy and human-robot collaboration.

My research focuses on human-aware planning, behavior trees, and verification and monitoring for autonomous robots. I completed my PhD in Bioengineering and Robotics at the University of Genoa and have also worked at the University of Birmingham.

I work mainly with ROS 2, YARP, C++, and Python. I’m interested in making robot behaviors practical, understandable, and dependable around people.
