Robotics engineer · Postdoctoral researcher

Hi, I’m Stefano.

I design the decision-making tools that help robots act reliably, adapt to changing situations, and work naturally alongside people.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Humanoid Sensing and Perception group at the Italian Institute of Technology in Genoa. My work sits at the intersection of robot planning, behaviour trees, and verification and runtime monitoring for autonomous systems.

With a background in computer science and engineering and a PhD in Bioengineering and Robotics from the University of Genoa, I turn research ideas into deployable robot software. I work primarily with ROS 2, YARP, C++, and Python, with experience spanning human-aware planning, museum guide robots, and human–robot collaboration.
